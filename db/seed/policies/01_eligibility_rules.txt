Relief Program Eligibility Rules

The mock relief program provides financial assistance exclusively to widows residing in eligible districts of Sindh province. All applicants must meet every condition listed below to qualify for a payout.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Marital Status: Applicant must be a widow. Only widows are eligible for this relief program. Divorced, separated, or single women do not qualify under this program.

2. Monthly Household Income: Total monthly household income must be PKR 30,000 or less. Household income above PKR 30,000 makes the applicant ineligible regardless of other conditions.

3. Eligible Districts: Applicant must reside in one of the following approved districts:
   - Karachi East
   - Karachi West
   - Karachi South
   - Thatta
   - Badin
   Applicants from any other district are not eligible for this program.

4. Payout Amount: Eligible widows receive a one-time relief payout of PKR 25,000. This amount is fixed and determined by the active program rules. No other amount will be paid.

If all four conditions are satisfied (widow status, income at or below PKR 30,000, eligible district, and valid identity documents), the applicant qualifies for the PKR 25,000 relief payout.
